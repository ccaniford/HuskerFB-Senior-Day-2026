Senior Day Ceremony Information

Senior Day

On Saturday, November 21, we will recognize our Nebraska Football seniors and celebrate their accomplishments and dedication to our program. The ceremony begins at 10:42 a.m. on the field at Memorial Stadium, where your son has given so much to the Huskers. We look forward to sharing this special moment with you and your family as we honor your son and his career at Nebraska.

Check-In Information

Check-in begins at 9:30 a.m. and ends at 9:45 a.m. Parents and participating family members will check in within the lobby of the Osborne Legacy Complex (OLC). Following check-in, families will gather in the OLC Atrium before proceeding to the Tunnel Walk area for the Senior Day Ceremony.

Family Members for Senior Day

We ask that only immediate family members join us for the ceremony portion of the game. If any family members taking part in the ceremony have ADA needs, please include them in your RSVP.

Senior Day Ceremony Day-Of Schedule

Nebraska vs. Ohio State, November 21, 2026. Check in at the lobby of the Osborne Legacy Complex (OLC).

Senior Day Ceremony / Celebration of US

Check-In

  • 9:30 a.m.Check-in begins within the lobby of the Osborne Legacy Complex (OLC).
  • 9:45 a.m.Check-in ends.

Staging in the Atrium

  • 9:50 a.m.Senior families move to the staging area within the OLC Atrium. Football staff will organize the order of families for the ceremony within the Atrium.

Tunnel Walk and Sideline

  • 10:21 a.m.Senior families head up the Tunnel Walk to stage for the Senior Day Ceremony.
  • 10:34 a.m.Senior families are escorted to the visitor sideline and stage on their specific yard marker for the Senior Day Ceremony.

Senior Day Ceremony

  • 10:42 a.m.Senior families head out to the hashmarks in the middle of the field. The Senior Day Ceremony begins.
  • 10:47 a.m.The Senior Day Ceremony ends. Senior players head to the locker room.

Celebration of US

  • 10:48 a.m.Senior families head to the Tunnel Walk to line up for the Celebration of US.
  • 10:50 a.m.Remaining Husker families begin lining the Tunnel Walk.
  • 11:05 a.m.Your Huskers depart the locker room and take the field!
  • 11:08 a.m.All families walk up the Tunnel Walk after the team and head to their seats.
