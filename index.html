Senior Day Ceremony Information

Senior Day

On Saturday, November 21, we will recognize our Nebraska Football seniors and celebrate their accomplishments and dedication to our program. We look forward to sharing this special moment with you and your family as we honor your son and his career at Nebraska.

Check-In Information

Parents and participating family members will check in at the Hall of Champions, located inside the Osborne Legacy Complex. Following check-in, families will gather in the atrium of the Osborne Legacy Complex before proceeding to the Tunnel Walk area for the Senior Day Ceremony.

Specific check-in and ceremony times will be provided once the official kickoff time has been announced.

Family Members for Senior Day

We ask that only immediate family members join us for the ceremony portion of the game. If any family members taking part in the ceremony have ADA needs, please include them in your RSVP.
